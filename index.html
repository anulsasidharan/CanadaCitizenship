📊 Political Hierarchy: The Chain of Command

👑 Canadian Monarch (King/Queen)
         ↓
🏛️  Governor General of Canada
         ↓
⚜️  Lieutenant-Governors (Provinces) / Commissioners (Territories)
         ↓
🏘️  Premiers (Provincial/Territorial Heads of Government)
         ↓
🗳️  Provincial/Territorial Legislatures & Assemblies

Remember: This hierarchy flows from the Crown at the top down through all levels of government!

🎓 Key Learning Points

🏛️ Provinces vs. Territories

  • Provinces (10): Have more legislative power, use Lieutenant-Governors
  • Territories (3): Use Commissioners, governed by consensus-based model

🎯 Major Political Parties (Colour Coded)

  • Conservative/UCP — Red
  • NDP — Orange
  • Liberal — Red
  • Progressive Conservative — Blue
  • Coalition Avenir Québec — Blue
  • Saskatchewan/Yukon Party — Green

🌟 Crown Representatives

  • All are appointed by the Crown (King/Queen)
  • Lieutenant-Governors in provinces wear formal purple attire
  • Commissioners in territories have similar ceremonial roles

📍 Natural Resources Pattern

  • Atlantic Provinces: Focus on fishing, forestry, mining
  • Prairies: Agriculture, natural gas, mining
  • Western: Oil, forestry, water
  • Central: Manufacturing, mining, tourism
  • Northern: Varied resources, smaller populations
